About Us

Who We Are

Practice & Liability Consultants are experienced practice management consultants with 25+ change to 35+ years of healthcare administration and consulting experience. Since 1985, we have worked with over 2300 practices of all sizes and specialties from solo to multi-specialty groups of over 100 physicians. The majority of clients are groups of under 10 physicians.

We have successfully met the credentialing criteria that includes physicians reference checks for the following organizations:

  • American Academy of Family Physicians – Charter Member
  • American College of Physicians – Managed Care Resource Center
  • American Academy of Ophthalmology
  • American College of Gastroenterology
  • The IPA Association of America
  • National IPA Coalition

In addition, our firm is one of the few consulting and seminar firms allowed to teach seminars and receive referrals from The California Medical Association in addition to several local medical societies in California.
This is due to the:

  • Quality of the seminars we present
  • Effectiveness of our consulting services in achieving desired results
  • Ethics in business we adhere to for our physician clients

It is a testament to our experience that our consultants are nationally recognized speakers to the medical profession. Please review our seminar sponsor list. Our consultants are dynamic seminar instructors chosen for their ability to communicate knowledge with humor and intelligence, excellent communication and teaching skills and ability to offer practical solutions to practice problems.

Why Hire Us

Our goal is for our consultation fee to pay for itself within one year after consultation. This has been achieved in the vast majority of client consultations. Additional benefits include: reduction of partner conflicts, increased staff morale, greater productivity of physicians and staff, increased gross and net income, greater patient and referral satisfaction and a more pleasant work environment. Fees are based on either a flat fee or hourly basis and where possible, a minimum and maximum fee is quoted. For local consultations, a complimentary meeting is arranged to discuss credentials and to acquaint the physicians with the consultant.
